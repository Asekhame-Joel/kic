Who We Are

A God-given mandate, birthed through divine instruction

Kingdom Impact Church is a God-given mandate, birthed through divine instruction, with a passion to raise men and women for Kingdom influence in every sphere of life. Since our first service in May 2024, we have seen lives transformed, destinies restored, and hearts ignited with the fire of God.

At KIC, we are committed to equipping believers with the Word and the Spirit to impact their world for Christ.

Raising Men, Influencing Spheres

Equipped for the home, the workplace, the campus and the community

We raise and equip men and women to lead in every area of life: home, workplace, campus, and community. Through discipleship, practical training, and Spirit-led ministry, we send out purpose-driven believers who influence their spheres for Christ.
