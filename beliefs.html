The Precepts (Tenets) Of Our Faith

Our Beliefs

We believe strongly in these statements below and hold on to them strongly.

Our MinistriesSalvation
  1. 01

    We believe in God the father of our Lord Jesus Christ.

    Ephesians 1:3; 1 Peter 1:3

  2. 02

    We believe God created Man.

    Genesis 1:26-27; Psalm 100:3

  3. 03

    We believe God made the heaven and the earth and everything that dwells therein.

    Genesis 1:1; Psalm 24:1-2; Isaiah 42:5

  4. 04

    We believe that Jesus Christ is the son of God.

    John 1:1-14; Hebrews 1:1-3; 1 John 4:15

  5. 05

    We believe that Jesus was conceived and born of a virgin.

    Matthew 1:18-25; Luke 1:26-38; Isaiah 7:14

  6. 06

    We believe in all the words and acts of Jesus in the scriptures.

    John 1:1-14; John 21:25

  7. 07

    We believe that Jesus came to the world, lived, died, was buried and resurrected on the third day for our justification from sin.

    1 Corinthians 15:3-4; Romans 4:25, 5:8

  8. 08

    We believe that Jesus Christ is the ONLY way to be saved.

    John 14:6; Acts 4:12

  9. 09

    We believe that Jesus is in heaven and would return to take the believers with Him.

    Acts 1:9-11; 1 Thessalonians 4:13-18; John 14:3

  10. 10

    We believe in the power of the blood of Jesus to cleanse and save.

    Romans 5:9; Ephesians 1:7; 1 John 1:7; Revelation 12:11

  11. 11

    We believe in the Holy Spirit.

    John 14:26; 1 Corinthians 3:16, 6:19-20

  12. 12

    We believe that the Holy Spirit is our seal.

    Ephesians 1:13-14; 2 Corinthians 1:22

  13. 13

    We believe that the Holy Spirit gives the ability to speak in other tongues.

    Acts 2:4, 10:46, 19:6; 1 Corinthians 12:10

  14. 14

    We believe in the power of the Holy Spirit.

    Romans 15:13; Acts 1:8

  15. 15

    We believe that the word of God (the scriptures) is the truth.

    John 17:17; Psalm 119:160; 2 Timothy 3:16-17

  16. 16

    We believe that the word of God is the true agent for transformation.

    Romans 12:2; Hebrews 4:12-13

  17. 17

    We believe that the scriptures is the standard for doctrine.

    2 Timothy 3:16-17; Matthew 22:29

  18. 18

    We believe in Miracles.

    Matthew 9:2-8; Mark 16:17-18; Acts 2:43

  19. 19

    We believe in healing.

    Matthew 9:35; Psalm 103:2-3; 1 Peter 2:24

  20. 20

    We believe that Faith is the way to please God.

    Hebrews 11:6; Romans 1:17; Galatians 3:11

  21. 21

    We believe in prayer and its potency.

    Matthew 7:7-8; Mark 11:24; 1 John 5:14-15

  22. 22

    We believe in the fellowship of the Spirit.

    2 Corinthians 13:14; Philippians 2:1-2

  23. 23

    We believe in fellowship with the brethren.

    Acts 2:42-47; Hebrews 10:24-25; 1 John 1:7

  24. 24

    We believe in Praise and Worship.

    Psalm 100:4; John 4:23-24; Revelation 5:11-14

  25. 25

    We believe there's a heaven where God is and Jesus reigns.

    Matthew 6:9-10; Revelation 4:1-11, 21:1-4

  26. 26

    We believe that love is the nature of the believer in Christ.

    1 Corinthians 13:1-13; Galatians 5:22

Do you believe with us?

Take the first step into a life of rest and peace in Jesus Christ, then grow with a church family that loves you.
