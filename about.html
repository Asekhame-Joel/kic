Our Journey

From eighteen people to a family in their hundreds

Kingdom Impact Church is a God-given mandate, birthed through a divine instruction. With little in the way of physical resources at the beginning, God has lavished the ministry with undeniable grace.

Since our first service in May 2024, with just 18 people in attendance, God has brought remarkable growth. In just one year, we have celebrated countless testimonies of salvation, deliverance from demonic influences, and transformed lives. Hundreds have surrendered their hearts to Christ, many have been filled with the Holy Spirit, and countless others have discovered their God-given purpose.

On May 25th, 2025, we joyfully celebrated our first anniversary with over hundreds of people in attendance. Today, Kingdom Impact Church is blessed with a loving family of committed members in their hundreds, seven active cell units, and a thriving new chapter at the University of Port Harcourt.

Our outreach efforts have touched many lives, including memorable outreaches where our neighborhood received gifts in cash, clothing, food, and more — spreading the love of Christ in practical ways.

At Kingdom Impact Church, we exist to see lives ignited with the fire of God, equipping Kingdom representatives to impact their world with the word of God and by His Spirit.

Milestones

  • May 2024 — First service held with 18 people in attendance.
  • May 25, 2025 — First anniversary celebrated with hundreds in attendance.
  • Today — Seven active cell units and a campus chapter at the University of Port Harcourt.

Our President

Reverend Nathan Ikenria

Reverend Nathan Ikenria is a seasoned minister of the Gospel with over a decade of faithful service under his man of God. In 2024, he was commissioned and sent forth to pioneer Kingdom Impact Church, which officially launched in May of that year. Since its inception, the ministry has been a place of salvation, love, impact, healing, and miracles, touching and transforming countless lives.

Through this ministry, Rev'd Nathan Ikenria has been instrumental in building and equipping believers to become true Kingdom representatives in their spheres of influence. Known as a passionate and insightful teacher of God's Word, his heart beats for seeing men and women transformed — spirit, soul, and body — by the power of the Holy Spirit and the truth of Scripture.

He is joyfully married to his beloved wife and is blessed with three wonderful children. Together, they are committed to advancing God's Kingdom and igniting the fire of revival wherever they go.
