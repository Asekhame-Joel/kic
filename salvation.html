Call To Salvation

Finding Peace in a Chaotic World

Are you searching for a refuge from the storms that rage around you? You're not alone.

Do you feel overwhelmed, burdened, and exhausted from the weight of life's challenges? Are you searching for a refuge from the storms that rage around you? You're not alone.

In today's world, chaos and trouble seem to lurk around every corner. The pressures of daily life can leave us feeling frustrated, confused, and desperate for a respite. But amidst the turmoil, there is a promise of hope and rest.

Taking The First Step

The Prayer of Salvation

If you're ready to surrender your burdens to Jesus and receive His rest, pray this prayer of salvation:

“Dear Lord Jesus, I come to You today, acknowledging my need for Your salvation. I believe you are the son of God. I believe you died on the cross for my sins and rose from the dead for my justification. I confess you as my Lord and savior, I receive your eternal life. Thank You for Your love and mercy. Amen.”
